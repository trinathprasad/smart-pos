Feature Guides

The Dashboard gives a quick view of active products, low-stock products, today’s revenue and profit, recent sales, sales trends and top-selling products.

  1. Review the summary cards for today’s activity.
  2. Use the sales chart to review trends and the top-products chart to see what sells most.
  3. Select a product or low-stock summary link when you need to investigate further.
Open Dashboard

Products stores the item details used by billing and inventory, including SKU, barcode, category, unit, prices, stock quantity and low-stock threshold.

  1. Search by product details and use category, stock-status and sorting controls.
  2. Use Edit to update product information.
  3. Use Add Product to create a new item. SKU and barcode values must not duplicate existing products.

Restriction: Products used in sales are archived rather than physically deleted.

Open Products

Categories organize products and can have an optional validated image.

  1. Open Categories and choose Add Category.
  2. Enter a unique category name.
  3. Optionally upload a valid PNG, JPG or JPEG image up to 2 MB.
  4. Open a category to see its active products.

Restriction: A category with active products assigned to it cannot be deleted until those products are reassigned.

Open Categories

Customers stores contact details and maintains a ledger of pending amounts, payments and bill activity.

  1. Search customers by name or phone, then open a customer record.
  2. Use Add Pending, Receive Payment or Clear Pending from the customer detail page.
  3. Review Balance History and Bill History to understand the current balance.

Validation: Customer names use letters and spaces, phone numbers must contain exactly ten digits, and a payment cannot exceed the pending balance.

Open Customers

New Bill creates a sale, records payment information and reduces stock for each valid line item.

  1. Select active products and enter a positive quantity no greater than available stock.
  2. Choose Paid, Pending or Partial and enter any payment reference.
  3. Review the totals and save the bill to open the invoice.
  • Paid: the full payable amount is recorded as paid.
  • Pending: no payment is recorded.
  • Partial: the paid amount must be greater than zero and less than the total payable.
Create New Bill

Inventory focuses on active stock monitoring. It shows stock by unit, low-stock and out-of-stock counts, purchase value, and the current product list.

  1. Search by product name or SKU.
  2. Filter by category or stock status.
  3. Use Edit, Adjust Stock or History for the selected product.
  4. Use the export or print actions when you need a report.

Status rules: Out of Stock is stock less than or equal to zero; Low Stock is above zero and at or below the alert threshold; Healthy is above the threshold.

Open Inventory

Stock Adjustments record physical stock changes without changing billing logic.

  1. Open Inventory and choose Adjust Stock for an active product.
  2. Choose Increase Stock, Decrease Stock or Set Stock.
  3. Enter a valid quantity and a reason, then review the resulting stock.
  4. Save the adjustment.

Validation: Increase and decrease quantities must be positive, Set Stock may be zero, and the resulting stock cannot be negative. Archived products cannot be adjusted.

Choose a Product in Inventory

Stock History records the adjustment type, previous quantity, quantity changed, new quantity, reason and timestamp for a product.

  1. Open Inventory and choose History for a product.
  2. Filter by Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days or a custom date range.
  3. Filter by adjustment type or search the reason text.

History uses local calendar dates and excludes future-dated movements from the unfiltered view.

Choose a Product in Inventory

Daily Reports summarize bills for a selected local calendar date.

  1. Choose a date and select View.
  2. Review bills, sales, tax, outstanding balance, profit and top items.
  3. Use Export CSV for the selected date.
Open Daily Reports

Shop Settings controls the shop information and invoice defaults used by billing and printed or downloaded invoices.

  1. Update the shop name, phone, address, invoice prefix, tax percentage and footer note.
  2. Optionally upload or remove the shop logo.
  3. Use Invoice Preview to review the invoice appearance before creating a bill.

The invoice page supports browser printing and PDF download.

Open Shop Settings

Data & Backup manages local copies of the file-based SQLite billing database.

  1. Choose Create Backup.
  2. Review the backup entry and its validation status.
  3. To restore, choose a valid backup and confirm the replacement.

Before restore, the application creates and validates a safety backup. Backup files are checked for SQLite integrity and required application tables.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a product marked Low Stock?

Its stock is greater than zero and less than or equal to its low-stock alert threshold.

What is the difference between Low Stock and Out of Stock?

Out of Stock means stock is zero or negative. Such products are not counted as Low Stock. Low Stock requires stock above zero.

Why can’t I decrease stock below zero?

Stock adjustments prevent a negative resulting quantity. Check the physical quantity and use Set Stock when completing a count.

What do Paid, Pending and Partial mean?

Paid records the full payable amount, Pending records no payment, and Partial records an amount greater than zero but less than the total payable.

How is customer pending balance maintained?

Customer ledger debit and credit entries track manual dues, payments, cleared balances and customer-linked bills.

Why can’t I delete a category?

Categories with active products assigned cannot be deleted. Reassign those active products first.

What happens when I delete a product with sales history?

It is archived instead of physically deleted, and its SKU receives an archive suffix so historical sales remain safe.

What is Inventory Value?

It is the purchase price multiplied by current stock for active products. Stock by unit is shown separately because pcs, kg and litres are not interchangeable.

How do I create a backup?

Open Data & Backup and select Create Backup. The local SQLite copy is integrity-checked and added to Backup History.

What happens before restoring a backup?

A safety backup is created and validated first. The selected backup must also pass integrity and required-table checks.

Does a database backup include uploaded shop and category images?

No. The backup implementation copies the SQLite database only. Uploaded shop and category images are stored as files under the static uploads area and are not included in the database backup.

Troubleshooting

Product not appearing in billing

Confirm that the product is active. Archived products are excluded from billing and product search.

Insufficient stock during billing

The requested quantity is greater than current stock. Reduce the quantity or adjust the stock after verifying the physical count.

Duplicate SKU or barcode

Use a unique SKU and barcode. The product form rejects values already assigned to another product.

Invalid customer phone number

Enter exactly ten digits. Names must contain letters and spaces, and an active customer phone number cannot be reused.

Category image upload validation failure

Use a real PNG, JPG or JPEG image no larger than 2 MB. The extension, MIME type and image contents are checked.

Stock adjustment rejected

Check the adjustment type, use a valid quantity with at most two decimal places, provide a reason, and ensure the resulting stock is not negative.

Backup creation or validation failure

Confirm that the application uses a file-based SQLite database and that the backup directory is available. Only validated backups are listed as restorable.

Backup restore rejection

Restore accepts only application backup filenames in the configured backup directory that pass integrity and required-table validation. Confirm the restore action when prompted.
